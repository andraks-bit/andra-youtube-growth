Frequently Asked Questions

What's the cheapest way to rent a boat in Marbella?

Our smallest day boats start at €230 for 2 hours. For larger groups, Astondoa 40 and Azimut 39 offer excellent value at €749 for 2 hours, including skipper.

Is a skipper always included?

Yes. Every boat includes a licensed skipper. This means no boating license required on your part.

Do you operate year-round?

Yes. Available year-round on the Costa del Sol. Off-season (Nov–Mar) offers better pricing and fewer crowds.

How far in advance should I book?

Peak season (June–August, weekends): 2–4 weeks. Shoulder season: 1 week. Off-season: 2–3 days possible.

Can children come on board?

Absolutely. Many families charter with us. We provide life jackets. Smooth morning trips from Puerto Banús are ideal for young children.

What happens if weather is bad?

Your skipper makes the call. If unsafe, you reschedule with full refund—no penalties.
